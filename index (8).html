Who we are

More Than Just an English Class.

A community where students learn English through practice, creativity, friendship, and meaningful experiences.

Our story
“English is not only a subject. It is a bridge to opportunities, knowledge, and the world.”

English Club SMA 11 Wajo is a student learning community designed to help members develop communication skills and confidence in using English in everyday situations.

Whether you are a beginner or already enjoy speaking English, you are welcome to learn, practice, and grow with us.
